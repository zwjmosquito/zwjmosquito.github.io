Finally rode the full East Sammamish Trail, just before it officially closes in May.

I didn't expect to go that far. My only goal was: ride a little beyond my furthest point.

But somewhere along the way, I kept going. And then suddenly, Issaquah was right there in front of me.

A small goal turned into a surprising adventure.
