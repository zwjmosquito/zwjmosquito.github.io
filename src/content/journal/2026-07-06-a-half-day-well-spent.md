title: "A Half Day Well Spent"
date: 2026-07-06
excerpt: "A stolen half day, the 520–Lake Washington Loop, and a pure moment of happiness."
---

I stole a luxurious half day from the long weekend and rode the 520–Lake Washington Loop.

The best part came on a 10% descent, cruising at 28 mph with 洪卓立《少年游记》in my ears. When the music, the speed, and the wind all came together, I couldn't help but smile. It was one of those simple, pure moments of happiness that reminds me exactly why I love riding.
