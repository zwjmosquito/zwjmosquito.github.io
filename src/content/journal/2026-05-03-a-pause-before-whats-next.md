Last day before a new chapter begins.

There's a quiet mix of excitement and nervousness. Like standing at the edge of something unknown, but meaningful. The past few weeks have been a rare kind of luxury, with long rides and open time that I know won't come as easily again. Still, I hope I can keep riding whenever I can, even if it looks different.

Starting over always takes courage.
