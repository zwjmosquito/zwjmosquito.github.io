date: 2026-05-23
excerpt: "A memorable and unexpected birthday in Orange."

A memorable and unexpected birthday in Orange. As part of a business trip that was never planned that way.

Spent it with colleagues I've worked with for years but never met in person before, along with many new faces just beginning to know. Everyone carries their own pressure, uncertainty, and challenges. There were many moments I questioned myself too. Whether this was the right move, whether everything ahead would work out.

But being around this group reminded me why I took the leap in the first place. The people I work with gave me confidence, inspired me, and made me believe in what we're building together. I've learned so much from them over the years, and now I find myself passing those same values and energy on to my own team as well.

Maybe that's what leadership truly is, not having all the answers, but bringing people together in a way that makes everyone feel stronger.
